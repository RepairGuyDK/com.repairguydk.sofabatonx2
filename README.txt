SofaBaton X2 brings your universal remote into Homey over MQTT. Homey can press any key on any device in your SofaBaton - TV, amplifier, aircon, fan, projector - straight from a Flow, and every MQTT button on the remote can start a Flow in return, so one press on the couch can dim the lights, close the curtains and roll the film.

The hub is found automatically on your network and the broker settings are filled in for you, so all you need is an MQTT broker that both the hub and Homey can reach - the free MQTT Broker community app running on Homey itself will do. Tap Refresh devicelist on the device and your devices and keys show up as ready-made dropdowns in the Flow editor. This is an unofficial community app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SofaBaton.
